About

I'm a PhD researcher at the Advanced Technology Development Centre (SRRF Lab), IIT Kharagpur, working in computational materials science — studying how materials behave at the electronic and molecular level using density functional theory and molecular dynamics. My PhD work has focused on the temperature-dependent elastic properties of apatite ceramics (hydroxyapatite and carbonated fluorapatite), including benchmarking DFT exchange correlation functionals and developing displacement-field-based methods for estimating elastic constants. More recently I've been working on how spatially localized electric fields at metal surfaces reshape molecular adsorption — the CO/Cu(100) project featured above.

I did my M.Sc in Physics at Jadavpur University and my B.Sc (Honours) in Physics at Calcutta University. Outside research, I enjoy quoting Einstein on curiosity more than is probably healthy.
